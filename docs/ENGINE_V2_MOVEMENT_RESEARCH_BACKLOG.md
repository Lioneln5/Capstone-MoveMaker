# Engine V2 movement and retention research backlog

**Status:** deferred, non-blocking research. This is not a release-blocking
register and does not authorize additional feature searches by default.

## Required before candidate promotion

### Meaningful Retention final validation

- Current state: development candidate, not deployed.
- Reopen when: a fully mature, player-disjoint, untouched temporal cohort is
  large enough for the frozen pooled and league-level gates.
- Do not: reuse the exposed or underpowered 2024 cohort as a pristine final
  test, or relabel contribution-only probability as retention.

### Temporary Displacement final validation

- Current state: development candidate, not deployed.
- Reopen when: the same later temporal requirements can be met with enough
  temporary events in every advertised subgroup.
- Do not: treat rolling-development performance as final validation.

## Deferred research

### Permanent Separation repair

- Current state: personalized output unavailable.
- Known failure: negative development-stage Brier skill in Bundesliga and
  Ligue 1 despite positive pooled skill.
- Reopen when: a materially new decision-time-safe mechanism is available and
  can be frozen before outcome testing.
- Plausible future routes: player-relative financial status, promotion
  transition, time-safe injury availability, club competitive trajectory, or
  a justified hierarchical league/club structure.
- Already tested without repair: club finances, lagged movement and extension
  history, role trajectory, squad competition, manager stability, base
  formation stability and manager-transition role change.
- Do not: apply manual league, superstar, age, market-value, goal or club
  bonuses; weaken subgroup gates; or repackage generic continuity.

Generic continuity is retired. It is not a backlog item and should not be
