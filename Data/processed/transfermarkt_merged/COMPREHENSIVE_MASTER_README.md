# Transfermarkt comprehensive transfer master

Generated by `scripts/build_transfermarkt_comprehensive_master.py`.

The master contains 35,139 rows and 577 columns at exactly one row per `transfer_event_id`. No transfer is removed for missing features or outcome maturity.

## Modular inputs

- `transfer_core.csv`: transfer facts and historical player/club identities.
- `transfer_valuation_features.csv`: as-of and 12/24-month valuation fields.
- `transfer_appearance_features.csv`: career, pre-365, and post-transfer appearance aggregates.
- `transfer_lineup_features.csv`: pre-transfer and destination selection aggregates.
- `transfer_event_features.csv`: goals, cards, shootouts, substitutions, and assists from event records.
- `transfer_club_context.csv`: origin/destination pre-transfer form and destination outcome coverage.

## Timing rule

The three historical layers are `pre365` (recent), `pre730` (medium-term), and `career_pre` (all recorded history before transfer). The 365-day layer is also used as a recency/eligibility gate; it does not truncate older history. Columns containing `post12` or `post24` are outcomes or diagnostics and must never be model features.

## Known limitations

Movement type remains unresolved; zeros are not classified as free transfers. Market-value change is a proxy, not accounting ROI. Snapshot-labelled player fields are references rather than historical as-of attributes. Source-supplied transfers outside the observed game/valuation date ranges are retained and explicitly flagged.
