About Move Maker

Move Maker is a football contract-extension decision tool built as a data analytics capstone project. It evaluates proposed terms for a player at their current club by combining historical extensions, wages, playing involvement, public market value, club context, and comparable-player evidence.

Instead of producing one vague “success score,” Move Maker separates future contribution, movement and meaningful-retention research, public-value downside, and historical fixed-wage context. The original all-purpose continuity output was retired after later diagnostics showed that temporary displacement, permanent separation, and meaningful sporting use could not be defended as one mechanism.
