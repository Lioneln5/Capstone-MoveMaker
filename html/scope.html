The supported use

MoveMaker is a frozen analytical case study of incumbent-club contract-extension risk. It examines which questions public historical data could support before an existing player's extension. It does not issue a current player score or make the contract decision.

A sporting director would need to combine any research evidence with internal medical information, scouting, tactical plans, squad depth, clauses, negotiation leverage and club finance.

What survived the final audit

  • Retained descriptive context: exact or explicitly labelled contract arithmetic, salary percentile and share of the known public wage panel.
  • Qualified research: repaired sustained realized contribution, Temporary Displacement Risk and 12-month public-value downside.
  • Definition retained, model unfinished: Meaningful Retention—no outbound movement plus at least 25% same-club opportunity share in each of Year 1 and Year 2.
  • Secondary development evidence: 24-month public-value downside, whose later cohort is small and previously exposed.
  • Historical-only: contract-duration, fixed-commitment and wage-to-public-value peer benchmarks from frozen V1.

What was retired, rejected or withheld

  • Generic club continuity was retired because it blended temporary loans, loan returns and permanent exits into one misleading stability interpretation.
  • Permanent Separation is unavailable because discrimination was weak and league-level behavior was unreliable.
  • The annual-wage candidate was rejected because Ligue 1 range coverage failed the predeclared Big-Five gate even though the pooled result appeared acceptable.
  • The integrated contract score was rejected because it performed below the opportunity component alone and hid disagreement between the modules.

Claims the case study does not support

  • An overall good-deal, bad-deal, compatibility or “success” score.
  • Evaluating a player for a new or hypothetical destination club.
  • Tactical or cultural fit, injury risk, motivation or manager preference.
  • Exact future minutes, tenure, departure date, transfer fee or sale proceeds.
  • An optimal wage, optimal contract length, accounting loss, expected profit, expected loss or return on investment.
  • Complete contract cost: bonuses, taxes, agent fees, release clauses, club options and other negotiated terms are not consistently observed.
  • Betting advice or individual financial advice.

Binding data and evidence limitations

Contribution: the repaired target measures realized involvement, not pure player quality or tactical role. It requires evidence in both future years and adequate club-schedule coverage. La Liga, Serie A and midfield did not clear the strict final subgroup gate.

Movement: Temporary Displacement has encouraging development evidence but no sufficiently mature, player-disjoint, untouched final cohort. Permanent Separation lacks reliable cross-league signal.

Public value: market value is a public estimate, not an audited asset value or guaranteed transfer price. Available 2024+ outcomes had already been scored during earlier verification and cannot become a pristine final test. The 24-month player-disjoint 2024 cohort contains only 26 rows, with three in its smallest league group.

Wages and contracts: public salary panels can be incomplete and are not audited payroll. Fixed wages exclude bonuses, taxes, agent fees, clauses and options. Historical ranges describe observed peers; they are not fair-value or affordability recommendations.

Current authorization

Engine V1 is frozen for audit. HTTP scoring is unconditionally disabled, no Engine V2 probability is authorized, and the site cannot be switched back into live-scoring mode through an environment setting. The retained outputs are evidence for a case study—not a club-ready production system.
