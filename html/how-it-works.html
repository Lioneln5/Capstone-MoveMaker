01 · Canonical pipeline
Six stages protect the meaning of each row.
01

Preserve sources

Clean each provider independently while retaining source IDs, dates and provenance.

02

Resolve identities

Map players and clubs through stable IDs, normalized names, reviewed aliases and explicit unresolved states.

03

Anchor extensions

Deduplicate Capology records into one player–club–date event and validate join cardinality.

04

Build pre-date features

Join prior wages, public value, performance, playing time, injuries and club context without crossing the signing date.

05

Construct outcomes

Use exact anniversary windows for contribution, dated valuation landmarks and future transfers for movement.

06

Audit every build

Emit coverage checks, cohort funnels, field dictionaries, manifests, hashes and refusal states.

Performance was not enough. The research also checked calibration, improvement over the chronology-only baseline, player-clustered uncertainty, origin consistency, league/position/age behavior, player-disjoint evaluation where available, out-of-scope refusal, logical relationships and artifact parity. The final freeze fits no model; it reconstructs the reported conclusions from saved evidence.
