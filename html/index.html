What it shows

“Sustained meaningful contribution” means playing at least 25% of the maximum club minutes available in both Year 1 and Year 2. The score is the probability of clearing that threshold in both seasons—not a talent or tactical-fit rating.

Financial translation

Low-contribution exposure equals fixed wages scheduled through 24 months × the probability of failing the 25% threshold in either year. It is a prioritization proxy, not expected loss or a claim that those wages will be wasted.

Decision use

A club can compare contract lengths or wage proposals and ask whether guaranteed salary is concentrated beyond the player’s likely contribution window.

Model basis

The model uses age, position, league, market value, recent and prior-season involvement/performance, proposed terms and available wage context from historical extensions.

What it shows

“Continuous stay” means no departure of any kind during the period. A loan counts as an interruption; a player can therefore remain under contract while failing this stricter continuity definition.

How it is calculated

A discrete-time survival model estimates departure risk across successive intervals, then converts those hazards into the probability of an uninterrupted stay through 24 and 36 months.

Decision use

Compare the likely relationship horizon with the guaranteed wage horizon. Large scheduled wages beyond Month 36 deserve stronger exit, loan and succession planning.

Important distinction

This is not a loyalty or performance score. Strategic loans, planned sales and demand from other clubs can all reduce continuity.

What it shows

The model predicts whether a public market-value estimate crosses a defined downside threshold. The euro figure translates that threshold; it is not a predicted sale loss.

How it is calculated

Separate calibrated models estimate 25% and 50% downside events using the player’s pre-extension sporting, market, contract and club context. The selected probability is paired with current public value.

Decision use

A high probability and large euro threshold can justify shorter terms, stronger option structures, deeper injury/form diligence or a lower guaranteed wage.

Important distinction

Public market value is a transparent proxy, not the club’s accounting carrying value, a guaranteed transfer fee or realized resale proceeds.

What “commitment” means

This is financial commitment only: proposed annual fixed wage × contract years. It does not measure playing time, effort, contribution, market value, or expected loss.

How it is calculated

The model estimates historically typical total fixed wages for players with a similar age, position, league, public market value, and prior wage when known. The proposal is then subtracted from that central estimate and plotted against the historical 80% range.

How to read the chart

The central peer marker is the model estimate—not the midpoint of the broad range. The proposal marker shows the entered deal. A proposal can be above the central estimate while still remaining inside the uncertainty range.

Not a fair-value verdict

The benchmark describes comparable historical behavior. It does not know bonuses, bargaining leverage, agent terms, clauses or strategic importance.
